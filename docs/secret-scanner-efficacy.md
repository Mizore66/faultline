# Secret-scanner efficacy (SEC-04)

Labeled corpus study for FaultLine's best-effort text redaction / snapshot
secret boundary. Coverage is intentionally **LIMITED**.

## Corpus

Path: `benchmarks/secret-scanner-corpus/`

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `true_secret` | Should be redacted (HIGH-confidence documented formats) |
| `false_positive` | Benign-looking text; redaction counts as FP |
| `should_miss` | Known limitation cases (encoded / split) — missing is expected |

Labels: `labels.json`.

## Method

1. Build: `pnpm build`
2. Run: `pnpm measure:secret-scanner`
3. For each labeled file, call `redactText` from `src/redaction.ts`
4. Record TP / FN / FP / TN and known-limitation miss behavior
5. Publish raw JSON: `benchmarks/secret-scanner-efficacy.json`

## Measured rates

Rates are regenerated by the script. Do not hand-edit invent numbers — re-run
the measurer after corpus changes. The committed `secret-scanner-efficacy.json`
is the last retained measurement.

## Limitations

- Only documented credential formats and secret-named fields are recognized.
- Encoded, split, encrypted, binary, or transformed credentials may remain.
- Digests of redacted spans support correlation; they are not encryption.
- This is not a general DLP product evaluation.

## Non-claims

- Not a guarantee of zero secret leakage in snapshots or ledgers.
- Not comparable to commercial secret scanners without shared corpora.
